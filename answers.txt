(a) 
Pros of Agile Development:
    1) An iterative approach that can used to keep adding new things
    2) Brings constant value to customer by have a working feature per iteration

Cons of Agile Development:
    1) Time and costs spent per iteration is difficult to predict
    2) Overall stages and full completion of project never set

Pros of Non-Agile Development:
    1) The requirements are fixed from the start so no need to be off track
    2) Completed within one iteration

Cons of Non-Agile Development:
    1) Does not give a usable product to the consumer until the end
    2) Testing is delayed and might not unveil issues until later

(b) 
    Roles and Responsibilites of Scrum Master
        The Scrum Master is in charge of handling all the scrum related tasks such as using 
        tools on JIRA (sprints, epics, etc.), overseeing sprints and hosting meetings

    Roles and Responsibilites of Product Owner
        The Product Owner sets the team in the right direction and handles releases of the product
        and manages stakeholders.

(c) Three types of meetings in a sprint cycle
    [1] Sprint Planning Meeting 
        - Plan what will be completed in the upcoming sprint
        - Move existing stories from the backlog into the current sprint
    [2] Daily Scrum / Standup Meeting
        - Discuss work completed per team member
        - Plan work for the day and discuss potential blockers
    [3] Sprint Retrospective Meeting
        - Discuss work that has been completed
        - Move work that is incomplete into the backlog

(d)
    Epics: Contains a large amount of work that can be broken down into smaller stories
    Story: Software system requirement that is explained using a few short sentences
    Bug: Smallest unit of work to be done to fix a feature that is broken
    Task: Administrative or internal requirement that is explained using a few short sentences
